# Sample photos

The photos templates show before a host adds their own: previews, the editors and the Template Studio
(`@bulava/template-schema` sample-photos.ts chooses them by occasion and tradition). Every one is free to
use in Bulava without payment or attribution: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) or public domain, from the
sources below. Each was re-encoded to WebP (at most 1200 px on the long side, metadata removed); the ones
marked *cropped* show part of the original.

The Muslim and Sikh couples are shown by their hands and wedding clothes for now. When licensed photographs
of a nikah couple and an Anand Karaj couple are available (for example from Pexels through the stock
settings' API key), replace `muslim-couple`, `muslim-bride`, `muslim-groom`, `sikh-couple`,
`sikh-bride` and `sikh-groom` here and in apps/admin/public/samples under the same names (and update their
sizes in sample-photos.ts and these credits).
